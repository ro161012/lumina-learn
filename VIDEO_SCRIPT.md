# Lumina — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

**Total Runtime:** 2:00  
**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover  
**Tone:** Enthusiastic, clear, confident

---

## [0:00–0:15] Hook + Problem Statement

**SCREEN:** Lumina landing page (empty state)

**NARRATION:**
> "Every day, students spend hours reading textbooks and lecture notes — then forget 70% within 24 hours. The problem isn't information overload. It's passive reading. What if you could turn any text into an interactive study session in seconds? Meet Lumina."

---

## [0:15–0:30] Core Value Proposition

**SCREEN:** Click "+ New study set", show the paste form

**NARRATION:**
> "Lumina takes any text — lecture notes, textbook chapters, articles — and instantly generates flashcards, quizzes, and an AI tutor that answers your questions. No account required. No data leaves your device."

---

## [0:30–0:50] Flashcard Generation

**SCREEN:** Click the Photosynthesis sample → flashcards appear → click a card to flip it → rate it

**NARRATION:**
> "Let's try it. I'll load a sample on photosynthesis. Lumina's NLP engine analyzes the text and generates 15 cards in under 2 seconds — cloze deletions, definitions, and Q&A pairs. Click to flip, then rate yourself: Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. The spaced repetition algorithm schedules reviews right before you'd forget."

---

## [0:50–1:10] Quiz Mode

**SCREEN:** Click "Quiz" tab → answer a question → show instant feedback

**NARRATION:**
> "Switch to quiz mode for multiple-choice questions with instant feedback. Get it right? Great — Lumina adapts the card's interval. Get it wrong? It comes back sooner. This is active learning, not passive reading."

---

## [1:10–1:30] Neural AI Tutor

**SCREEN:** Click "Ask" tab → type a question → show the answer with confidence score and citation

**NARRATION:**
> "Here's where it gets interesting. Lumina runs real neural networks — right in your browser. A sentence-embedding model finds the most relevant passages, then a RoBERTa-based QA model extracts the exact answer. No API keys. No servers. Everything runs offline via WebAssembly."

---

## [1:30–1:45] Concepts + Progress

**SCREEN:** Click "Concepts" tab → show keyword cloud → click "Progress" tab → show mastery stats

**NARRATION:**
> "The Concepts tab maps your material with a keyword cloud and ranked search. The Progress tab shows mastery percentages, card maturity, and your review behavior over time."

---

## [1:45–2:00] Closing + Call to Action

**SCREEN:** Back to flashcard view, show the Lumina logo

**NARRATION:**
> "Lumina turns passive reading into active learning — powered by real AI, 100% private, 100% free. Paste your notes, flip some cards, and learn anything, faster. Thank you."

---

## 🎬 Production Notes

- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 (or 1280×720 for smaller file)
- **FPS:** 30
- **Audio:** Clean voiceover, no background music (or subtle lo-fi)
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts between sections
- **Cursor:** Show cursor movement, but keep it smooth
- **Typing:** Type at normal speed (don't fast-forward)
- **Models:** The AI models download on first use — record this after they're cached for a smooth demo
